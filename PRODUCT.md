# MedCUA product context

## Purpose
MedCUA is a research initiative on computer-use agents for medical workflows. It is the umbrella for MedOpenClaw and MedFlowBench, with a separate task-contribution questionnaire. The public site explains the research direction, presents released work and invites real clinical or research tasks.

## Users
Clinicians and medical researchers assess relevance and share workflows. AI researchers inspect the runtime, benchmark, evidence and publications. Collaborators find the team and contact information.
