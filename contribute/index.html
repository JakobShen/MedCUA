Before you start

Tasks we are looking for

We are looking for long-tail workflows from real clinical or research practice. These tasks may be specialized, variable, or infrequent, and lack a dedicated automated solution. We are particularly interested in the following characteristics.

  • 01

    Time-consuming or repetitive

    Especially when many manual interactions are required.

  • 02

    Limited existing solutions

    Not well solved by existing models or standard pipelines.

  • 03

    Multi-step

    For example, segmentation, measurements, visualization, comparison, quality control, or interpretation.

  • 04

    Multiple tools or data

    Tasks may involve multiple software tools, modalities, or time points.
